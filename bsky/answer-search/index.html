How it works

Ettin-17m turns your query into a vector and your GPU searches the posts for the closest ones. Then Ettin scores every sentence of those posts against the query, and the best sentence from each of the top posts is shown, best match first. Nothing is generated, so nothing can be misquoted.

Model and engines measured 2026-11-03 with compression on; the index sizes are today's snapshot. A GitHub Action rebuilds the snapshot every morning: it asks Bluesky for the list's new posts, embeds them with the same Ettin model, and drops the oldest beyond 100,000. The index uses 1 bit per dimension: against exact search it keeps 9.3 of the 10 quoted sentences, where 2 bits kept 9.8, at half the size (the measurement). This tab then asks Bluesky's public API for the posts written since the snapshot, embeds them on your GPU, adds them to the same index, and checks for new ones every minute while the tab is visible. Your browser keeps a snapshot for two days and fills in the gap itself, so a daily visitor downloads the index every other day. Your query and the results never leave this tab; the only requests after the download are those for new posts.

Ettin-17m distilled for Bluesky search (ettin-native), a hand-written WGSL engine, and a GPU scan over remex codes, all shared with Three query embedders. Needs WebGPU (Chrome, Edge, Safari 26+).

An earlier version also had Monad, a 56-million-parameter model, write an answer from the top posts. 1 of its 18 quotes was word for word; the rest were stitched from pieces or made up, so it was dropped.
