Bluesky List Scout

Find accounts that post about a topic and are not on your list yet. It searches Bluesky posts for your terms, learns the topic from the posts it finds, reads each author's last 100 posts, measures on your GPU how much of it is on topic, and lets you pick who goes on a list.

Sign in

Bluesky only answers post searches for signed-in accounts. Use an app password, not your main one; the session is kept in this browser, as on the site's other list tools.

How it works

Search terms are searched as phrases over the last N days, newest first. Authors who are not on the lists you skip, and who turn up at least the minimum number of times, are sampled: their last 100 posts and replies, without reposts. Ettin-17m embeds posts on your GPU. To learn the topic, it embeds up to 1,500 of the posts the search found and 1,200 posts from Bluesky's Discover feed, and a logistic regression fitted in the tab tells the two apart; the log reports its AUC on a fifth of the posts, held out from fitting. That model then scores every sampled post, and an account's topic share is the mean of its posts' scores. Tested on 200 members of an AI list against a classifier trained on posts Claude Haiku labelled about AI or not, it ranked them almost the same way (rank correlation 0.89 learned from three terms, 0.93 from forty) but gave shares 0.08 to 0.2 higher, so read the share as a ranking and set the pre-tick threshold by looking at the table. Nothing leaves this tab except the requests to Bluesky.

Grown from the experiment that built an AI list this way: bsky-list-ai-share. Model and WebGPU engine shared with Three query embedders and Bluesky Answer Search. Needs WebGPU (Chrome, Edge, Safari 26+).
