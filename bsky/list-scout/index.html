Find accounts that post about AI and are not on your list yet. It searches Bluesky posts, reads each author's last 100 posts, measures on your GPU how much of it is about AI, and lets you pick who goes on a list.

How it works

Search terms are searched as phrases over the last N days, newest first. Authors who are not on the lists you skip, and who turn up at least the minimum number of times, are sampled: their last 100 posts and replies, without reposts. Ettin-17m embeds every sampled post on your GPU, and a logistic regression trained on 600 Bluesky posts labelled about AI or not (cross-validated AUC 0.88) scores each one. An account's AI share is the mean of its posts' scores, the same measure used to decide who to remove from the list it was built for. The classifier only knows "about AI"; other search terms still find people, but the share column will not measure their topic. Nothing leaves this tab except the requests to Bluesky.

Method and the measurement behind it: bsky-list-ai-share. Model and WebGPU engine shared with Three query embedders and Bluesky Answer Search. Needs WebGPU (Chrome, Edge, Safari 26+).
