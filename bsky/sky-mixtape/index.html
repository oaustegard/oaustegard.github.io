Sky Mixtape

Turns your last Bluesky posts, or a few keywords, into a playlist. A small language model reads the posts in your browser and matches them against 247 moods, genres, themes and settings; the strongest matches pick the songs. Thumbs up and down are saved as records in your own Bluesky repo and steer every later mix.

1. Bluesky

Sign-in uses Bluesky OAuth and asks only to write Sky Mixtape records (ratings and saved mixes). You can make a mix without signing in; ratings then stay in this browser.

2. What the mix is made from

Reads public posts, including image alt text and link titles; reposts are skipped.

Anything goes: a mood, a place, a topic, a scene.
