Tilt Rally

Turn on your phone’s rotation lock in portrait, then turn the phone a quarter turn clockwise and hold it in both hands, like a steering wheel.

  • Turn the phone to steer
  • Tilt the top away for gas
  • Tilt it toward you to brake

Three laps of a mountain valley circuit against three cars. However you hold the phone when you press Start engine is straight ahead with no gas, so hold it the way you want to drive. Double-tap the screen to re-center. On a keyboard: arrows or WASD.
