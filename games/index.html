Games

Arcade games for the browser, many steered by tilting your phone, some running on WebGPU

Order
  • LemmingsLemmings in WebGPU compute: one small kernel per lemming state, and 10,000 lemmings at once
  • Tilt InvadersSpace Invaders steered by tilting your phone: divers, motherships, a march on the beat
  • Prism BreakBrick breaker steered by tilting your phone: glass bricks shatter in WebGPU light, upgrades, a synth score
  • Asteroids 3DFirst-person Asteroids in WebGPU: tilt to steer, tap to fire, slide to thrust or brake
  • gPu(doom)The Doom engine as WebGPU compute shaders: BSP rendering and the game tic run on the GPU
  • Lantern EelSnake in the deep sea, steered by tilting your phone; the light fades the deeper you dive
  • ScoopMinesweeper with dog poop: sweep the lawn, bag the heaps, try not to step in one
  • SoundingMinesweeper on a WebGPU sea floor: sonar pings, glowing beacons, a chain reaction on a miss
  • Tilt ChompPac-Man-style maze chase steered by tilting your phone, drawn in WebGPU with synth sound
  • PlumbFalling blocks steered by tilting your phone, over WebGPU dust that pools under real gravity
  • Ball MazeTilt your phone to roll a steel ball through endless generated mazes and around holes
  • BlasterSpace shooter against descending saucers, with sound effects synthesized in Web Audio
  • AsteroidsAsteroids with keyboard, touch or tilt steering, levels and lives, written in Preact
