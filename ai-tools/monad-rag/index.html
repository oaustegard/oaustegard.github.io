Monad Bluesky RAG

A whole retrieval-augmented generation system in this tab, 98 MB in all. Ettin-17m turns your question into a vector and your GPU searches 61,686 Bluesky posts about AI for the closest ones. Then Monad, a 56-million-parameter reasoning model, answers from those posts and quotes them. Monad is small, so the quotes are checked: each one is marked by whether it is really in the post it cites. Most are stitched together from pieces.

Settings
Monad was trained (on SYNTH) with up to ten numbered sources. More posts leave less of its 2,048-token window for thinking, and the lowest-ranked posts are dropped if they would leave under 640.

How the quotes are checked

Monad cites a post by writing <ref name="source_2">…</ref> around a quote, as SYNTH taught it. Each quote is compared with the posts, ignoring case and punctuation:

✓ word for word: the quote is in the post it cites.
↷ moved: the quote is in a different post than the one it cites.
≈ stitched: runs of six or more words come from the posts, but no post has the whole quote.
✗ not found: none of it is in any post.

Click a mark to jump to the post. On six test questions, 1 of Monad's 10 quotes was word for word and 9 were stitched. A stitched quote can say the opposite of the post: "I think it's dumb to blame data centers for hogging electricity" came back as support for "data centers are energy-efficient infrastructure".

Retrieval: Ettin-17m distilled for Bluesky search (ettin-native), hand-written WGSL engine, and a GPU scan over remex 2-bit codes, all shared with Three query embedders. Generation: PleIAs/Monad (Apache-2.0) with the training-free loop fix from Editing Monad's weights to stop its loops, on onnxruntime-web 1.30, shared with Monad Bullshitter. Needs WebGPU (Chrome, Edge, Safari 26+).
