Bluesky Answer Search

Ask a question and get answers quoted from Bluesky posts about AI, word for word, each with its author and a link. Everything runs in this tab from a 28 MB download. Ettin-17m turns your question into a vector and your GPU searches 61,686 posts for the closest ones. Then Ettin scores every sentence of those posts against the question, and the best sentence from each of the top posts is the answer. Nothing is generated, so nothing can be misquoted.

Settings, and the generator experiment
At most one sentence per post, best match first.
Monad is a 56-million-parameter reasoning model trained to answer from numbered sources and quote them. It gets the top five posts, and each quote it writes is checked against them: ✓ word for word in the post it cites, ↷ in a different post, ≈ stitched from runs of words in the posts, ✗ in no post. In tests, 1 of 18 quotes was word for word. A stitched quote can reverse the post: "I think it's dumb to blame data centers for hogging electricity" came back as support for "data centers are energy-efficient infrastructure". That is why the answer above is extracted rather than written. With Monad on, the whole system is 98 MB.

After the download nothing is sent anywhere: your question, the search and the answer stay in this tab. The posts are from members of the "Artificial (and real) Intelligence" list, collected in October 2026.

Ettin-17m distilled for Bluesky search (ettin-native), a hand-written WGSL engine, and a GPU scan over remex 2-bit codes, all shared with Three query embedders. Monad: PleIAs/Monad (Apache-2.0) with the loop fix from Editing Monad's weights to stop its loops, on onnxruntime-web 1.30, shared with Monad Bullshitter. Needs WebGPU (Chrome, Edge, Safari 26+).
