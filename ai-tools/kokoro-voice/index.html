Kokoro Voice

Type anything and Muninn reads it aloud. Kokoro-82M text-to-speech runs on your own device, each word lit as it is spoken, and nothing you type leaves the page. The first visit downloads an 88 MB model; after that it loads from this device.

Pronunciation

Words come from misaki's British English dictionaries (about 200,000 entries). A word in neither is guessed from dictionary words that spell it, or left silent, and the page names it. To set how a word is said, write [word](/phonemes/) in Kokoro's phoneme alphabet, for example [Muninn](/mˈuːnɪn/). [word](+2) raises its stress and [word](-2) removes it. Device, speed and storage

The model is Kokoro-82M v1.0 (Apache-2.0) from kokoro-onnx's ONNX export, shrunk to 102 MB (int8 where it only shapes the sound, fp16 where it sets timing and pitch) with its STFT corrected to match PyTorch. It runs in onnxruntime-web. Text becomes phonemes through a JavaScript port of misaki (Apache-2.0).

How it was built and measured: kokoro-web and kokoro-onnx-kinescript. Muninn's voice is a 2:1 blend of Kokoro's bm_fable and bm_lewis.
