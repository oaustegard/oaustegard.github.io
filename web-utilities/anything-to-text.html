Anything to Text

Drop an image, a PDF, an audio/video recording, or a document (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, EPUB, HTML, LaTeX…) and get text back. Images and PDFs are read with on-device OCR; audio and video are transcribed with a local speech model; documents are converted to Markdown by pandoc. Your files never leave your device — only the models download on first run.

Before a page is read, a PP-OCRv6 or Tesseract model checks its orientation on a reduced copy. Text lines that come out tall mean a sideways page; a reading that is clearly more confident when turned half a way round means upside-down. Indra turns pages itself, so with Indra alone this does nothing. Wrong guess? Use the ↺ ↻ buttons on the page card.
