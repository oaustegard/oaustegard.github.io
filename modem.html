Acoustic Link

16-MFSK, 1.5–4.3 kHz, ~187 bps · a third signaling channel for filedrop-manual: no camera, no QR, no list · single file, zero dependencies

Send — speaker

Receive — microphone

gold bar = pilot (1200 Hz) · teal bars = the 16 data tones

Self-test — no audio hardware needed

encode → add 15 dB-SNR noise → decode, entirely in RAM

Notes

Demo on one machine: press Listen, then Send — the mic hears the speaker across the air gap between them. Two machines: one listens, the other sends.

Frame format: 250 ms pilot · 8-symbol sync · 2-byte length · payload (2 symbols/byte) · CRC-16/CCITT. A ~200-byte packed SDP is about 9 seconds of audio.

Validated headless (Node invariant suite): clean loopback at 48 k and 44.1 k, random silence padding, AWGN to 8 dB SNR (decodes to 3 dB), 48 k → 44.1 k resample mismatch, 25% gain wobble, and all three combined; pure noise never false-decodes (CRC + sync gate).

Browser-path (cannot be tested headless): mic requires HTTPS and a user grant; the getUserMedia constraints disable echo cancellation / noise suppression / AGC, but some stacks ignore them — OS-level noise suppression can eat the tones; AudioWorklet loads from a Blob URL and falls back to ScriptProcessorNode automatically; speaker volume around 60–80% works best; decode passes cost ~100–250 ms of CPU each while listening.

Known v1 limits: no FEC yet (CRC + resend is the protocol) — a Hamming(7,4) layer is the obvious next change, one hypothesis at a time. Range is conference-room scale, not hallway scale.

Deploy: single self-contained file, zero dependencies — drop modem.html at the site root; GitHub Pages serves it at /modem.
