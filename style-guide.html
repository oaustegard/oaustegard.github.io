Map

The map is Kartverket open data for the Flatøy area, redrawn as SVG in the site’s inks. Austegarden is marked at 60.5373° N, 5.2690° E, from the place-name register (Austegarden, type Bruk, a farm holding; stedsnummer 842304, its place-name ID). N50 is Kartverket’s 1:50 000 map series, FKB its detailed base map data and AR5 its land-cover layer; DTM is the digital terrain model. WMS and WCS are the web services that deliver map images and elevation grids.

Light and darkBoth palettes sit in the SVG’s own <style> under prefers-color-scheme. Strokes are non-scaling, so line weights are in screen pixels at any size.
Marker and namesThe SVGs hold no marker and no text. On the home page the survey marker and the place names are HTML laid over the sheet in plane pixels (home.css).
Left outMarshes, farmland, built-up tint, depth contours, rivers, tunnels, ferries. Footpaths appear on the inset only. Roads on the sheet are N50 and generalized, so some lanes are missing.

Needs numpy, scipy, shapely, pyproj, scikit-image, tifffile, lxml and pillow, and network access to Kartverket and Geonorge only. Raw downloads are cached in --cache (default $FLATOY_CACHE or /tmp/flatoy-cache), never in the repo. The place labels and survey marker in index.html are registered to the sheet in plane pixels; if the extent changes, they move with it. Check the file sizes against the budget below after a run.

Rules

  • Performance budget. Home page, cold: HTML, CSS and JS together at most 95 KB; fonts at most 150 KB; no third-party requests; no layout shift; no main-thread task over 50 ms. The map is decorative and never blocks text: sheet at most 150 KB raw and 45 KB gzip, band 30 KB, inset 40 KB (now 113 KB, 26 KB and 38 KB).
  • Tool pages stay self-contained. Most tool pages carry their own CSS and do not link style.css. Any change to the shared CSS must keep every page that does link it looking right, and the legacy token names keep working.
  • No new fonts. Three families, all local; no Google Fonts and no icon fonts.
  • Attribution on map pages. Kartdata © Kartverket (CC BY 4.0) in the footer of every band and full page.
