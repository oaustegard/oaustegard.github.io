🕳️ Ball Maze

Tilt your phone to roll the ball from the top of the maze to the green goal. Earn a point for every stretch of ground you cover, and a big bonus for finishing each maze. From level 2 on, holes sit right in your path — hug the wall to skirt around them, or the ball drops in and goes back to the start.

Endless levels, generated fresh every time — each maze bigger and meaner than the last. How far can you get?

No tilt sensor? Use arrow keys / WASD, or drag on the board.
