Fun & Games

Games, generative toys and phone gadgets: a space shooter, a ball maze, fractals, a generative radio.

  • Ball MazeTilt your phone to roll a steel ball through endless generated mazes and around holes
  • Fractal DiveEndless WebGL2 zoom into a Julia set or an Apollonian gasket, with an automatic dive mode
  • Strudel FMGenerative radio: a program writes house, techno, lo-fi, ambient or dub, four bars at a time
  • Age Multiplier CalculatorTwo birthdates in, current age ratio out, plus the future dates it falls to 2x, 3x and so on
  • AsteroidsAsteroids with keyboard, touch or tilt steering, levels and lives, written in Preact
  • BlasterVertical space shooter against descending saucers, with sound effects synthesized in Web Audio
  • Cadence for DummiesCount steps or pedal turns over a time, get cadence in BPM and a Spotify playlist prompt
  • Car DashboardPhone HUD from GPS: speed, heading, altitude, grade and trip stats, in mph or km/h
  • Car Dashboard (demo)A looping simulated drive through the car dashboard, so it works without GPS
  • Catch-Up CalculatorLate for a group ride: time and distance to catch up, the speed you need, and a watts estimate
  • CurrentsFull-screen WebGL flow field of warped noise that follows the cursor; click changes the palette
  • Currents in MotionPhone-steered WebGL flow field: spin, tilt and roll pick the colour; shaking speeds the flow
  • EML CalculatorRewrite exp, ln and arithmetic as trees of one operator, eml(x,y) = exp(x) - ln(y)
  • Emoji Collage MakerTurn an uploaded image into a grid of colour-matched emojis; download as PNG or SVG
  • Emoji Collage Maker (categories)The collage maker with checkboxes to limit emojis to categories such as faces or nature
  • EpicyclesType a word; a chain of rotating circles traces it in one line, using a Fourier transform
  • Generative Typography PlaygroundRandom poems from word fragments, set as flow, cascade, scatter or spiral over a particle field
  • Infinity ShaderFull-screen WebGL shader animation after an equation by XorDev; scroll to zoom
  • Pelican, BicycleA 3D pelican in a straw boater pedals a bicycle along a beach; drag to look, scroll to zoom
  • Snooker Break Simulator2D snooker physics: rearmost-red break against Shaun Murphy's third-red break, with aim and spin
  • Spectral TreeEigenvalue paths of a matrix blend as a rotatable 3D tree; click a branch tip for its numbers
  • SpeedometerGPS speed in large mph digits for a windshield HUD; tap to mirror, double tap to rotate
  • Strudel ComposerRedirects to a claude.ai artifact that turns English descriptions into Strudel music code
  • tap.localizerTrain a k-nearest-neighbour model to tell where on the phone you tap, from accelerometer data
  • Unicorn SpotterInstallable map app for unicorn sightings; it runs on generated fake sightings, no backend
  • Video About Making VideosA 75-second narrated video that is a live web page: every frame is HTML, CSS and SVG
  • Webcam Emoji MirrorYour webcam feed redrawn live as a mirrored grid of emojis
  • Yin-Yang ImpactsTwo balls bounce inside a yin-yang and dent its boundary like liquid; hold to speed up
