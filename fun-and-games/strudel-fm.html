How the station decides

No model picks anything here. Every four bars a small program writes the next phrase as Strudel code, and Strudel plays it with real drum-machine and piano samples. The rules:

  • Arc. Sections follow a weighted chain: intro → groove → lift → build → drop → breakdown, with limits (a drop lasts at most two phrases, a build always resolves). Energy tilts the weights.
  • Harmony. The mood picks a mode (dark: aeolian or phrygian; deep: dorian; bright: ionian, mixolydian or lydian). Chords are the mode's own triads, chained with functional-harmony weights (V goes home, vi moves to IV or ii). A phrase leading into a drop ends on a dominant. Progressions repeat for a few phrases before changing.
  • Voicing. Each chord takes the inversion that moves the fewest semitones from the last one.
  • Bass. Style and energy pick a rhythm; the notes are the chord's root, fifth, octave, and a step toward the next root at the end of the bar.
  • Melody. A two-bar motif per phrase, answered and varied; on-beat notes snap to chord tones, off-beat notes walk the scale.
  • Drums. Layers enter with energy (kick, backbeat, hats, open hats and ride). The last bar before a bigger section gets a roll or a tom fill.
Built on Strudel 1.2.6 (AGPL-3.0), loaded from jsDelivr. Drum samples: tidal-drum-machines; piano: dough-samples. Sounds load from GitHub the first time you press POWER.
