What this is, and what it is not

Every name on the map is a person listed as currently in space by Launch Library 2. Each station's position is computed in your browser from its public orbital elements (SGP4, the standard model for this) and redrawn about twenty times a second. Footprints are placed along the last 94 minutes of each station's track and stay where they fell; the dotted line ahead is the next 94 minutes. The shaded night follows the real Sun.

  • Everyone aboard a station is in the same place. The parallel lanes of footprints are decoration, so that eleven walkers can be told apart.
  • Which station someone is on is inferred from their agency: CNSA astronauts to Tiangong, everyone else to the ISS. Launch Library's expedition rosters lag launches by days, so they were not used. Anyone who launched in the last day is flagged, since they may still be on the way up.
  • "Overhead" means above 10° elevation from Austegarden (60.54° N, 5.27° E). It says nothing about whether the station is sunlit or visible.
  • This is a toy. Do not use it to schedule anything that matters.
