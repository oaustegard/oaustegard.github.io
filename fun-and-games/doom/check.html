gPu(doom) GPU check

This page compiles every GPU kernel of gPu(doom) on this device, one at a time, and reports which ones fail and why. It takes a minute or more, then times a few seconds of real game tics on E1M1, stopping at once if the GPU is dangerously slow. Copy the report at any point, even while it runs, and send it back. It is saved as it goes, so it survives a reload.

Press "Run the check".
